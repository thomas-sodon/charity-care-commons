What is in it

  • fields: one row per compiled policy field, with its value as JSON, status (verified, provisional, abstained, disputed), quote, page and document sha256.
  • crosschecks: each comparison with the IRS Schedule H filing and its outcome.
  • facilities and policies: the facility universe and the policy versions that govern them.

Provisional fields have not been checked by a reviewer. The data never contains information about patients or households.
